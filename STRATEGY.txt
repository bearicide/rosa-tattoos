BRITNEY ROSA - FINAL SHOWPIECE STRATEGY
======================================

Core move:
Do not present this as “only two drawings.”
Present it as a focused two-piece artist introduction.

Museum/gallery framing:
- Title the two works.
- Write wall-label copy.
- Explain what each drawing proves.
- Connect both drawings to a larger tattoo identity.
- Make the site feel curated, not empty.

Positioning:
Britney Rosa is a Flint, Michigan tattoo artist and illustrator specializing in
cartoon-inspired tattoos, whimsical creatures, expressive character design,
and personal story-driven artwork.

Best public-facing line:
“Cartoon tattoos for people who want their story drawn with a pulse.”

Launch plan:
1. Post both drawings as a two-piece showcase.
2. Say this is the start of a cartoon tattoo / flash direction.
3. Invite low-pressure inquiries:
   “Send me a creature, pet, cartoon memory, object, or weird little idea.”
4. Make 6 tiny flash pieces next:
   worm, bat, moon, campfire creature, flower, tiny drum.
5. Build the world, then sell appointments from the world.

Tone:
Confident, warm, artful, not desperate.
She does not need to announce insecurity.
The site does the confidence work for her.
